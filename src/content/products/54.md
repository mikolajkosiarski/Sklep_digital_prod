54 designs where cute meets dark: the coquette grunge aesthetic between innocent and intense. Every design is processed through a heavy threshold filter for a grainy, high-contrast look.

- Romantic animals: deer couples, kissing bunnies, swans, squirrels with hearts and cats.
- Dark feminine: Angel Baby, Mercy and melancholic portraits.
- Typography: Sorrow, Love, Blessed, Amore and gothic lettering.
- Noir details: eyes, lighthouses and birds on a wire.

Every design comes as a transparent PNG in 4K at 300 DPI and as a white-background PNG with a classic photocopy feel, plus a ready-to-use Procreate brushset. Made for tattoo artists working in blackwork, fine line and stick and poke, soft goth and coquette fashion brands, zine makers and collage artists.
