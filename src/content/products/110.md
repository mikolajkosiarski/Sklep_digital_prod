Not a themed pack but a full asset library: 110 threshold-processed graphics spanning ignorant doodles, dark nature motifs, femme fatale imagery and brutalist objects, all unified by a dirty xerox texture. Think of it as a grunge starter kit. Every piece works on its own or layered into larger compositions.

- Ignorant style, 30+ designs: stick figures, ironic icons, crude hands and childish symbols.
- Dark nature, 25+ designs: dobermans, snakes, barbed wire, thorns and textured skulls.
- Femme fatale and noir: smoking women, baroque frames, vintage portraits and cinematic silhouettes.
- Goth elements: daggers, roses, dark typography and ornamental details.

Every design comes as a transparent PNG in 4K at 300 DPI and as a white-background PNG, with a Procreate brushset. Use them for tattoo flash sheets, streetwear collections, zines and collages, band merch and alternative fashion.
