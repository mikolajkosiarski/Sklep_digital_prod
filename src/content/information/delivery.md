## Digital collections

Digital packs contain files, rather than a physical item. This website currently displays previews only. There is no paid download area or order delivery service here yet.

## Current file packaging

The product descriptions reflect the existing collection packaging:

- The 100-, 200- and 300-design packs use a delivery PDF containing an access link.
- The 25-, 55- and 121-design packs contain their PNG files in ZIP archives.

This describes the existing packs, not a promise about the future store's delivery method. The [file guide](../help/) explains the available formats.

## Before downloads open here

The final delivery information will specify how an order is confirmed, how files are accessed, when access becomes available, whether links expire and how to recover access. File sizes, required software and any compatibility limitations will be shown before purchase.

These details are still being prepared. No delivery time, account requirement or permanent download entitlement is promised by this preview.

## A missing or damaged file

If you already purchased a pack on Etsy, contact the seller through that order with the pack name and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Direct-store support details are pending on the [Contact](../contact/) page. The future [returns and complaints information](../returns/) will explain how problems with a purchase are handled.
