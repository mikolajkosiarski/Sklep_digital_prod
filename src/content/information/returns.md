## Before the store opens

This preview does not accept orders. The process below is an outline for the future store; the seller's verified contact details, return address and practical instructions must be completed before launch.

For an existing Etsy order, use the support route and terms associated with that purchase. This page does not replace them.

## Changing your mind

For ordinary distance purchases covered by Polish consumer law, consumers generally have 14 days to withdraw without giving a reason. The starting point depends on the contract: typically receipt for goods, and conclusion of the contract for digital content not supplied on a physical medium.

The final policy must explain the applicable time limit, how to send a withdrawal statement, a model withdrawal form, return arrangements, any return costs and reimbursement rules. Exceptions must be identified only where they actually apply.

## Downloadable content

Digital files are not automatically non-refundable. For paid digital content supplied without a physical medium, an exception to the withdrawal right depends on the required express prior consent, acknowledgement of the loss of that right, and confirmation of the agreement and consent in a form the customer can retain.

Any future immediate-download flow must implement those steps before delivery. A statement on this page or a general acceptance of terms is not a substitute for that process.

## Problems with a product

Withdrawal because you changed your mind and a complaint about a product that does not conform to the agreement are separate matters. An applicable withdrawal exception does not remove mandatory rights concerning non-conforming digital content or goods.

Before launch, this section must explain how to report missing, damaged, incorrect or incompatible content, the available statutory remedies, and the complaint-handling procedure. It must also distinguish faults from requirements clearly disclosed before purchase.

## Physical products

Physical pieces are currently concepts. Final return instructions must match the actual products, seller and delivery model.

Producing an item after an order is placed does not, by itself, establish that it is a personalised item excluded from withdrawal. Any exception for goods made to a customer's specification must be assessed against the actual offer.

## Contact and next steps

The [contact page](../contact/) will hold the verified support details. To help resolve a future issue, useful information may include the order reference, affected product and a description of the problem. This is not intended to impose extra conditions on statutory rights.

Available out-of-court complaint procedures must be confirmed for the actual seller. The former EU Online Dispute Resolution platform closed in July 2025 and is not an available complaint route.
