## About this store

VISAMORE is preparing a store for digital artwork and selected physical products. This preview does not accept orders or payments and does not deliver purchased files. Physical pieces shown as concepts are not available to order.

These terms are being prepared for that future store. They do not replace the terms of an existing purchase made through Etsy.

## Seller and contact details

Before sales open, this section must identify the seller: legal name, registered or business address, applicable registration details, email address and telephone number. A separate address for complaints must be provided if it differs from the business address.

The [contact page](../contact/) will provide the verified details in one place.

## Products and compatibility

Each digital product page must describe the included files, number of designs, formats, dimensions and relevant software or device requirements. PNG artwork and Procreate brushsets are different formats; availability of one does not imply that both are included.

Physical product pages must state the final materials, sizes, relevant product and manufacturer information, availability and delivery conditions. Concept previews are not final product specifications.

The scope of permitted use of digital artwork will be set out in the [license](../license/) and linked before purchase.

## Ordering, prices and payment

To complete before launch: supported countries and currencies, applicable taxes, payment methods, when an order becomes binding, and the steps for reviewing and correcting an order.

The final checkout must show the total payable amount and any additional costs before the customer submits an order. Its confirmation control must clearly communicate the obligation to pay. An order confirmation and the relevant terms must be provided in a form the customer can retain.

## Delivery and access

To complete before launch: how files are delivered, when access is provided, whether an account is needed, any download or access limits, and support for delivery problems.

Physical delivery regions, methods, charges and estimated fulfilment times must be confirmed separately. This preview makes no delivery-time or lifetime-access promise.

## Withdrawal and complaints

The [returns and complaints page](../returns/) separates changing your mind from a problem with the supplied product. Downloadable content is not automatically excluded from withdrawal rights merely because it is digital.

The final terms must explain the applicable rights, how to exercise them, the complaint process, and available out-of-court dispute-resolution options. Mandatory consumer rights remain applicable.

## Website services

To complete before launch: the scope of any account, checkout or other electronic service, its technical requirements, rules for use, termination conditions and complaint procedure. Unlawful content must not be submitted through any future service.

The final version must also identify its effective date and the approach to future changes, without retrospectively changing completed purchases.

## Reference

This outline follows the information categories described by [UOKiK for distance sales](https://prawakonsumenta.uokik.gov.pl/prawo-do-informacji/sprzedaz-poza-lokalem-i-na-odleglosc/) and the [government guide to online-store terms](https://biznes.gov.pl/pl/portal/00146). Seller-specific details remain to be completed before trading.
