## Can I buy here yet?

Not yet. This website is a preview of the future Visamore store. You can explore the collections and their sample artwork; checkout and file downloads are not available here.

## PNG or Procreate brushes?

PNG files are images you can place in an application that supports the format. A `.brushset` contains brushes for Procreate. They are different ways of working with a design.

The 100-, 200- and 300-design packs include a Procreate brushset. The 25-, 55- and 121-design packs contain PNG files without a brushset. Check the **Files** and **Procreate** details on each product page.

[Read the PNG and brushset guide](../journal/png-or-brushset/) for background transparency and import instructions.

## What does a collection include?

Each product page lists the number of designs and file formats. Transparent and white-background versions are alternative versions of the artwork, not extra unique designs.

The images shown on the website are previews. They are not the complete downloadable pack. See [Delivery & downloads](../delivery/) for the current packaging information.

## Can I use the artwork commercially?

The license for purchases from this future store is still being defined. This preview does not grant a commercial-use license. For an existing Etsy purchase, refer to the terms provided with that order.

The [Usage license](../license/) page will describe the permitted uses before sales open here.

## Are the clothes available?

The [Physical collection](../physical/) currently shows apparel concepts and mockups. They are not available to order. Production details, measurements, materials and delivery options will be added before any physical product is sold.

## I need help with an existing purchase

For an order placed on Etsy, use the help or messaging options attached to that order. That keeps the purchase details with the original transaction.

The [Contact](../contact/) page will provide the direct store's support details once they have been confirmed.
