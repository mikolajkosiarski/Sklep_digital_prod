## The current preview

This is a static store preview. It has no checkout, customer account or newsletter signup. Contact and comment forms offer a local preview only: entered details are not sent to a server or saved by the site. The cart saves the selection in this browser.

Fonts and artwork are served with the site. No analytics or advertising integration is configured in the current project.

This description applies to the preview, not to a future deployed shop with payments and downloads.

## Who is responsible for your data

To complete before launch: the identity and contact details of the data controller, and any other contact information required for the actual business. The [contact page](../contact/) will provide the verified details.

## Data and purposes

Hosting may involve processing request information such as IP addresses and server logs. The hosting provider, purposes and retention arrangements must be confirmed before public deployment.

For the future store, this policy must describe the data actually needed for purchases, file delivery, support, accounting and any optional account. Each purpose must have a stated legal basis. Optional marketing must be addressed separately if it is introduced.

## Providers and international transfers

To complete before launch: the hosting, payment, email, download-delivery and other providers actually used; their roles; the recipients or categories of recipients; and any transfers outside the European Economic Area with the applicable safeguards.

No payment provider or analytics service has been selected in this policy.

## Retention

To complete before launch: a retention period or a meaningful criterion for each category and purpose, including support correspondence, orders, accounting records, access logs and any account data. A single indefinite retention statement is not sufficient.

## Your rights

The final notice must explain applicable rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability, as well as how to make a request and how to complain to the competent supervisory authority. Where processing relies on consent, it must explain how to withdraw that consent.

It must also explain which information is necessary for a service, the consequences of not providing it, and any relevant automated decision-making. The scope of a right depends on the processing and legal basis involved.

## Cookies and similar storage

The current project does not configure cookies or browser storage for tracking. A consent banner is not added solely because a site exists.

Before adding analytics, advertising, embeds or other tools, their storage and data flows must be checked. Where consent is required, optional tools must wait for it, and declining or withdrawing consent must work. Technologies strictly necessary for a service explicitly requested by a visitor are treated separately.

## External links

Following a link to another website takes you to a service with its own privacy information. External links are different from embedding a third-party tracker directly in this site.

## References

The final notice must reflect the information requirements in [Article 13 GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj). Retention details should follow the [UODO guidance on specifying retention](https://uodo.gov.pl/pl/676/4260). Rules for storing or accessing information on a visitor's device are set out in [Article 399 of Poland's Electronic Communications Law](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2024/1221/text.html).
