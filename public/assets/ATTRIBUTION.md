# Asset attribution

Only media used by the current Visamore pages is included here. Earlier moodboards,
frame studies, unused font candidates and complete commercial download packs are
not part of this application.

- Visamore branding, collection previews, product listing covers and frame artwork
  come from the existing Visamore project. Source records are retained in
  `docs/sources/assets/`, outside the public asset directory.
- Frame 128 SVG parts embed the original PNG artwork and crop it with an SVG
  viewport. They do not depend on external PNG files.
- SVG texture tiles are the existing project’s procedural paper, snake and chalk
  camo textures.
- IBM Plex Mono is distributed under SIL OFL 1.1. The accompanying license is
  `fonts/plex-OFL.txt`, retrieved from the official Google Fonts repository:
  https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/ibmplexmono/OFL.txt
- IBM Plex Mono Medium (500), Latin and Latin Extended WOFF2 subsets, is served
  locally from `fonts/plex-medium-*.woff2`. Retrieved from the official Google
  Fonts stylesheet on 2026-10-26:
  https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=IBM+Plex+Mono:wght@500&display=swap
- Pinyon Script is distributed under SIL OFL 1.1. Its existing license is
  `fonts/pinyon-script/OFL.txt`. The site serves the unmodified font converted
  from TTF to WOFF2 for smaller downloads.

These source notes do not grant redistribution rights to the commercial Visamore
artwork or product packs. The application contains the selected previews used
by the site, not the complete customer downloads.
